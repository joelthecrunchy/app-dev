## About the Series

**Naruto** is a Japanese manga written and illustrated by *Masashi Kishimoto*. It ran in *Weekly Shonen Jump* from 1999 to 2014 and was adapted into an anime by Studio Pierrot. It follows **Naruto Uzumaki**, a young ninja who dreams of becoming the **Hokage**, the leader of his village, Konohagakure (the Hidden Leaf Village).

## Why I Like It

1. The story is about never giving up, even when others doubt you.
2. The characters grow a lot from the beginning to the end.
3. The fights are exciting and the friendships are emotional.
